# Community conduct

Be respectful, constructive, and welcoming. Harassment, threats, discriminatory language, doxxing, and unwelcome sexual attention are prohibited. Discuss ideas and code without attacking people. Respect privacy and differing experience levels.

Maintainers may edit or remove inappropriate content, warn participants, or restrict participation proportionately. Report problems privately to the repository owner once the project is published. Reports will be handled confidentially where practical; retaliation is prohibited.
